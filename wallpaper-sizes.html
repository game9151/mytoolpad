What size should a wallpaper be?

Use an image at least as large as the screen's native resolution in both directions. For an iPhone 17 Pro Max that is 1320 × 2868 pixels. Bigger is fine, because the device scales it down. Smaller gets stretched and looks soft.

Shape matters as much as size. Phones are tall (about 19.5:9) and most monitors are wide (16:9), so a photo made for one gets heavily cropped on the other. Sizes are each model's native panel resolution; models in the same family can differ slightly.

Frequently asked questions

What size is an iPhone wallpaper?

It depends on the model. Recent Pro Max models are 1320 × 2868 pixels, the iPhone 17 and 17 Pro are 1206 × 2622, and the iPhone Air is 1260 × 2736. A 1320 × 2868 image works well on all of them.

Can I use a 4K wallpaper on my phone?

A 3840 × 2160 image is wide, so on a tall phone screen only a narrow slice is shown and it is stretched to fill the height. Look for a portrait wallpaper instead.

Why does iOS zoom my wallpaper?

When Perspective Zoom is on, iOS enlarges the wallpaper slightly so it can move as you tilt the phone. A little extra resolution and space around the subject helps.

What size for a dual monitor wallpaper?

Add the widths together: two 1920 × 1080 screens side by side need 3840 × 1080. For two 2560 × 1440 screens use 5120 × 1440.
