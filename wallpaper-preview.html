Preview a wallpaper on any phone, tablet or monitor

Pick a device, drop in an image, and this tool shows how the wallpaper will sit on that screen, including what gets cropped. It also compares the pixels in your image with the pixels the screen needs, so you find out before you set it whether it will look soft or blurry.

Phones are tall and narrow, monitors are wide, and most photos are somewhere in between, so a wallpaper is almost always cropped. Drag the preview to choose the part you want to keep, or turn on the lock screen clock to see whether the subject collides with the time. Nothing leaves your device: the image is only read by your browser.

How to use it

  1. Drop an image onto the box, or try the sample.
  2. Choose your device, or enter a custom size in pixels.
  3. Drag to position the image. Read the verdict and the 100% pixel view underneath.
  4. Optionally download a copy cropped to the exact screen size.

Frequently asked questions

What resolution should a phone wallpaper be?

At least the phone's screen resolution in both directions, for example 1320 × 2868 pixels for an iPhone 17 Pro Max. Anything bigger is fine because the phone scales it down. If the image has fewer pixels than the screen, the phone stretches it and it looks soft.

Are my images uploaded?

No. The image is read and drawn by your own browser. It is never sent to a server, so it stays private and works with large files.

Why does my wallpaper look cropped on my phone?

Phone screens are tall and narrow, while most photos are wide. To fill the screen the phone crops the sides. Drag the image in the preview to pick which part stays, or switch to Fit to see the whole image with bars.

Is a 4K wallpaper good enough for a phone?

Usually yes if the image is also tall enough. A 3840 × 2160 landscape image is only 2160 pixels tall, so on a phone it is cropped and stretched to fill a 2796 pixel tall screen. Check the verdict for your exact phone.

Where do the screen sizes come from?

They are each model's native panel resolution in pixels. Models in the same family can differ slightly, so if your device is not listed, choose Custom size and type its resolution. iOS can also zoom a wallpaper a little when Perspective Zoom is on, so a bit of extra resolution helps.
