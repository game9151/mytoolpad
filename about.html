About.

Simple, fast, private tools.

MyToolPad is a small collection of free browser tools built for students and everyday use: calculators, a Pomodoro timer, notes, a to-do list, converters and more.

The idea is simple: tools should open instantly, work without an account, and not bury the page in pop-ups. Everything runs in your browser. Tools that remember things, like the to-do list, notes and budget tracker, save them on your own device only.
