Free percentage calculator: find X% of Y, increase and decrease

Use this percentage calculator to work out what X% of a number is, what percent one number is of another, and how much a value has gone up or down in percent. Results update instantly and nothing is sent to a server.

The core formula is simple: part = (percentage ÷ 100) × whole. For percentage change it is (new − old) ÷ old × 100. A positive result is an increase, a negative result is a decrease.

How to use it

  1. Pick the type of question (for example "What is X% of Y?").
  2. Type your two numbers into the fields.
  3. Read the answer, which updates as you type.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Divide the percentage by 100 and multiply by the number. For example 15% of 80 is 0.15 × 80 = 12.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, then multiply by 100. Going from 50 to 65 is (65 − 50) ÷ 50 × 100 = 30% increase.

How do I add or subtract a percentage?

Multiply by 1 + rate to add (×1.2 adds 20%) or by 1 − rate to subtract (×0.8 takes off 20%). This is how discounts and sales tax work.
