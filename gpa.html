Free GPA calculator for students

Add your courses, grades and credit hours and this GPA calculator works out your grade point average instantly. It supports the US 4.0 scale and the Australian 7.0 scale (HD, D, C, P, F), and your courses are saved in your browser so they are still there next time.

GPA is total grade points ÷ total credit hours, where each course contributes its grade value (on a 4.0 scale A = 4, B = 3, C = 2, D = 1, F = 0) multiplied by its credits.

How to use it

  1. Add each course with its grade and credit hours.
  2. Review the list and fix any entries.
  3. Read your GPA, which updates as you edit.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my GPA?

Multiply each course grade value by its credit hours, add those up, and divide by the total credit hours.

What is the difference between weighted and unweighted GPA?

Unweighted GPA uses the plain 4.0 scale. Weighted GPA adds extra points for honors, AP or IB classes. Check your school’s policy for the exact scale.

Are my courses saved?

They are stored only in your browser on your device. Clearing site data will remove them.
