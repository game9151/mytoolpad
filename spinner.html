Free spin-the-wheel decision maker

Add your options, spin the wheel, and let it pick for you. It is a quick way to choose where to eat, who goes first, a classroom volunteer, or a raffle winner, with no ads and no account.

Each option has an equal chance of being selected. Your option list is kept in your browser so you do not have to retype it.

How to use it

  1. Type your options into the list.
  2. Press spin and wait for the wheel to stop.
  3. Remove the winner or spin again.

Frequently asked questions

Is the wheel fair?

Yes. Every option has the same probability of being chosen on each spin.

How many options can I add?

You can add as many as fit comfortably on the wheel. Very long lists make the labels small, so keep it readable.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for and no ads.
