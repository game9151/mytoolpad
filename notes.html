Free private online notes: no account, auto-saves as you type

A simple notes app that opens instantly. Create multiple notes, search them, and every change is saved automatically as you type.

Your notes are stored in your browser on this device, not on a server, so they stay private. Because of that, clearing your browser data will remove them: copy anything important somewhere safe.

How to use it

  1. Create a new note.
  2. Type: it saves automatically.
  3. Use search to find an older note.

Frequently asked questions

Are my notes private?

Yes. They are kept in your browser’s local storage and never uploaded to our servers.

Will my notes sync to my phone?

No. They live only in the browser you wrote them in, since there is no account.

Can I lose my notes?

Clearing site data or using private browsing can delete them, so back up important text.
