Free GST calculator for Australia: add or remove 10% GST

Choose "Add GST" to turn an ex-GST price into a GST-inclusive total, or "Remove GST" to split a GST-inclusive amount into the price before GST and the GST itself. Australia’s GST rate is 10%, which is preset, but you can change the rate for other countries (for example 15% for New Zealand).

The maths is simple: adding GST is price × 1.10, and the GST inside a total is total ÷ 11. Amounts are rounded to the nearest cent. This is a general calculator, not tax advice. Check the ATO website or your accountant for how GST applies to your business.

How to use it

  1. Choose Add GST or Remove GST.
  2. Enter the amount. Change the rate only if you are not using 10%.
  3. Read the price before GST, the GST amount and the total.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add 10% GST to a price?

Multiply the price by 1.10. For example, $100 plus GST is $110, of which $10 is GST.

How do I take GST out of a total?

Divide the GST-inclusive total by 11 to get the GST amount, then subtract it for the ex-GST price. A $110 total contains $10 GST.

Do I need to charge GST?

Generally you must register for GST once your annual turnover reaches the ATO threshold ($75,000 at the time of writing). Check ato.gov.au for current rules.
