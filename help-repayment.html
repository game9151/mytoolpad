HECS-HELP repayment calculator for 2026-27

Since 1 July 2025, compulsory repayments only apply to the part of your income above the threshold, like a tax bracket. For 2026-27 the threshold is $69,528. Earn $75,000 and you repay 15c on each of the $5,472 above it, which is about $821 for the year.

The same thresholds cover HELP, VET Student Loans, Australian Apprenticeship Support Loans and the other study and training loans the ATO collects. Source: ATO repayment thresholds and rates.

How to use it

  1. Enter your expected repayment income for 2026-27.
  2. Add your current balance from myGov to see roughly when it will be paid off.
  3. Change the pay rise and indexation guesses to see how sensitive the payoff date is.

Frequently asked questions

What is the HECS-HELP repayment threshold for 2026-27?

You start repaying once your repayment income is above $69,528. Between $69,529 and $129,717 you repay 15c for each dollar over $69,528. From $129,718 to $186,050 it is $9,028 plus 17c for each dollar over $129,717. Above that it is 10% of your whole repayment income.

Does my HELP debt charge interest?

No interest, but the balance is indexed on 1 June each year. Indexation is now set at the lower of the CPI and the Wage Price Index.

How is it collected?

Your employer withholds extra from each pay once you tell them you have a study loan. The exact amount is worked out in your tax return, and anything over or under is settled then.

Are voluntary repayments included?

No. This shows compulsory repayments only. Extra voluntary payments reduce the balance and shorten the time to pay it off.
