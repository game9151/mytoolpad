Resize by

Resize and convert images in your browser

Set an exact size in pixels, scale by a percentage, or pick a preset such as an Instagram post or a YouTube thumbnail. You can also convert between JPG, PNG and WebP at the same time, for example PNG to JPG or WebP to JPG. Everything runs on your device, so there is no upload wait and no file size limit beyond your device's memory.

When you shrink an image a lot, the tool scales it down in steps, which keeps edges and text cleaner than a single jump.

How to use it

  1. Drop one or more images on the box.
  2. Choose pixels, percent or a preset, then a format.
  3. Check the new size next to each image and download.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert PNG to JPG?

Drop the PNG in, set Format to JPG and download. Transparent areas are filled with the background colour you choose, white by default.

What does Crop to fill do?

It fills the exact width and height you set by trimming the edges of the image evenly, so nothing is stretched. Fit inside keeps the whole image and may end up smaller on one side.

Will enlarging make my image sharper?

No. Enlarging adds pixels but not detail, so the result looks soft. The tool marks enlarged images so you know.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Resizing and converting happen in your browser on your device.
