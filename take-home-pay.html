Australian take-home pay for 2026-27

Enter your salary or hourly rate and see what lands in your account each week, fortnight and month. The calculator uses the 2026-27 resident tax rates, where the 16% rate dropped to 15% on income between $18,201 and $45,000, plus the low income tax offset, the 2% Medicare levy and, if you tick it, your compulsory HECS-HELP repayment.

How to use it

  1. Type your pay before tax and choose year, month, fortnight, week or hour.
  2. Tick the boxes if your figure includes super or you have a study loan.
  3. Switch the period above the result to see weekly, fortnightly or monthly pay.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is this?

It works out the tax you owe for the full year. Your payslip uses the ATO withholding tables, which round each pay a little differently, so your pay can be a few dollars off each period. Your tax return evens it out.

Is super taken out of my pay?

No. The 12% super guarantee is paid by your employer on top of your salary, unless your salary is a package that includes super. Tick the box if yours does.

What is not included?

The Medicare levy surcharge for people without private hospital cover, salary sacrifice, deductions, other offsets and second jobs. It also assumes you are an Australian resident for tax for the whole year.

What changed on 1 July 2026?

The lowest tax rate fell from 16% to 15% for income between $18,201 and $45,000, which saves up to $268 a year. It is legislated to fall again to 14% from 1 July 2027.
