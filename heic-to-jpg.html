Convert HEIC to JPG without uploading your photos

iPhones save photos as HEIC to save space, but Windows, many websites and some apps cannot open them. This converter turns HEIC and HEIF files into JPG or PNG right in your browser, so personal photos are never sent to a server.

Safari can read HEIC on its own. In Chrome, Edge and Firefox the page loads heic2any, an open-source converter, the first time you use it. Only that code is downloaded; your photos stay on your device.

How to use it

  1. Drop your HEIC photos on the box, or click to choose them.
  2. Pick JPG or PNG. JPG at 90% looks the same as the original for most photos.
  3. Download each photo, or use Download all.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The conversion runs on your device. The page only downloads the converter code, never your photos.

JPG or PNG?

JPG for photos: the files are much smaller. PNG keeps every pixel but files are often five to ten times larger.

How do I stop my iPhone saving HEIC?

Go to Settings, Camera, Formats and choose Most Compatible. New photos will be saved as JPG. Existing photos stay HEIC.

Is location data kept?

No. The converted file is redrawn from the pixels, so EXIF data such as GPS location and camera details is not copied across.
