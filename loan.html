Free loan calculator: monthly payment, total interest and amortization

Enter the loan amount, annual interest rate and term to see your monthly payment, total interest and total cost, along with a full amortization breakdown showing how each payment splits between principal and interest.

The standard payment formula is M = P × r(1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1), where P is the amount borrowed, r the monthly rate and n the number of monthly payments. It works for car loans, personal loans and student loans.

How to use it

  1. Enter the amount you want to borrow.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate and the term.
  3. Read the monthly payment, total interest and amortization table.

Frequently asked questions

How is a monthly loan payment calculated?

The calculator uses the standard amortization formula with your monthly interest rate and the number of payments, so each payment is equal.

Why is so much of early payments interest?

Interest is charged on the remaining balance, which is highest at the start, so early payments are mostly interest and later ones mostly principal.

Does a shorter term save money?

Yes. A shorter term raises the monthly payment but reduces the total interest you pay over the life of the loan.
