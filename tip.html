Free tip calculator and bill splitter

Enter the bill, choose a tip percentage and the number of people, and the tip calculator shows the tip amount, the total, and what each person pays. Pick a preset percentage or type a custom one.

The math is tip = bill × tip% and per person = (bill + tip) ÷ people. It is handy for restaurants, delivery, taxis and any bill shared with a group.

How to use it

  1. Enter the bill amount.
  2. Choose a tip percentage or enter your own.
  3. Set how many people are splitting the bill and read the per-person total.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I tip?

Customs vary by country and service. In the US 15–20% is common at restaurants; in many other countries service is included and a small round-up is enough.

How do I split a bill with a tip?

Add the tip to the bill, then divide the total by the number of people. The calculator does both steps at once.

Should I tip on the pre-tax amount?

Many people tip on the pre-tax subtotal. Enter whichever amount you prefer as the bill.
