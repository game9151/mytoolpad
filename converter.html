Free unit converter: length, weight, temperature, volume and more

Convert between common units of length, weight, temperature, volume, area, speed and data size in one place. Type a value, choose the units, and the result appears instantly.

Handy conversions include 1 inch = 2.54 cm, 1 kg ≈ 2.2046 lb, and °F = °C × 9/5 + 32.

How to use it

  1. Choose the category (length, weight, temperature and so on).
  2. Enter the value and select the from and to units.
  3. Read the converted result.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply by 9, divide by 5, then add 32. 20°C is 68°F.

How many pounds are in a kilogram?

One kilogram is about 2.2046 pounds.

Does it work offline?

Once the page has loaded, the conversions run in your browser, so they do not need a server.
