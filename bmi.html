Free BMI calculator: Body Mass Index for adults

Enter your height and weight and this BMI calculator returns your Body Mass Index and the standard category. It supports metric (cm, kg) and imperial (feet, inches, pounds).

BMI is weight in kilograms ÷ height in metres squared. The WHO adult categories are: under 18.5 underweight, 18.5–24.9 normal weight, 25–29.9 overweight, and 30 or above obese.

How to use it

  1. Choose metric or imperial units.
  2. Enter your height and weight.
  3. Read your BMI value and category.

Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy BMI?

For most adults a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered the normal range by the WHO.

Is BMI accurate?

BMI is a quick screening number. It does not separate muscle from fat or account for age, sex or body shape, so athletes and older adults may get a misleading result. It is not a diagnosis.

Can I use BMI for children?

No. Children and teenagers are assessed against age and sex growth charts, so ask a doctor or use a paediatric tool.
