QR.Code

Make a QR code for a link, text or Wi-Fi network. Download as PNG or SVG.

Free QR code generator with no watermark

Type a link or any text, or switch to the Wi-Fi tab to make a code guests can scan to join your network. Download the result as a PNG for screens and documents, or as an SVG that stays sharp at any print size.

Codes are generated in your browser and the text you enter is never uploaded. They do not expire and there is no tracking redirect, because a QR code simply contains your text. For print, keep strong contrast (dark code on a light background) and leave the white border around it.

How to use it

  1. Enter a link or text, or fill in your Wi-Fi details.
  2. Choose size, error correction and colors.
  3. Download the PNG or SVG, then test it with your phone before printing.

Frequently asked questions

Do these QR codes expire?

No. The code contains your text directly, so it works forever and there is nothing to renew.

What does error correction do?

Higher levels (Q, H) let a code still scan when part of it is damaged or covered by a logo, but make the pattern denser. Use M for most things.

PNG or SVG?

PNG is fine for screens and documents. SVG is a vector format, so choose it for signs, flyers and anything printed large.
