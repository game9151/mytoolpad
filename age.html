Free age calculator: your exact age in years, months and days

Enter a date of birth and this age calculator shows exactly how old someone is in years, months and days, plus the total days, weeks, months and hours lived and a countdown to the next birthday.

You can also change the "as of" date to find an age on a past or future day, which helps for school cut-off dates, forms, and eligibility checks. Calendar months and leap years are handled for you.

How to use it

  1. Pick the date of birth.
  2. Leave the "as of" date on today, or choose another date.
  3. Read the exact age and the next-birthday countdown.

Frequently asked questions

How is exact age calculated?

The calculator counts full calendar years since the birth date, then the remaining full months, then the leftover days, so month lengths and leap years are taken into account.

Can I find my age on a specific date?

Yes. Set the optional "as of" date to any day and the result shows the age on that date instead of today.

Is my date of birth stored?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
